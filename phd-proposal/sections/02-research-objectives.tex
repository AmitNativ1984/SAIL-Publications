\section{Research Objectives}
\label{sec:objectives}
This research works towards fast, agile flight of small unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), where onboard compute is severely limited and decisions must be made with low latency. To this end, the research pursues two objectives:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{End-to-end neuromorphic flight control.} Develop an end-to-end neuromorphic control stack, with an event camera and a spiking neural network (SNN) policy running on a neuromorphic processor, all onboard. The stack maps events directly to high-rate control commands for agile flight through unknown clutter. Compare it with a matched artificial neural network (ANN) in flight performance, latency and energy consumption.
    \item \textbf{Memory under partial observability.} Investigate whether the internal state of an SNN can act as a memory that improves control policies in partially observable Markov decision processes (POMDPs), where the onboard sensors observe only part of the state, for example flying with no velocity and position information.
\end{enumerate}
