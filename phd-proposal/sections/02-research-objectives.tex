\section{Research Objectives}
This research works towards achieving fast agiled flight in small unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), where low latency decision-making is required under severe power and computational constraints. To this end, the research focuses on the following objectives:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Develop a spiking neural network (SNN) based control policy for UAVs, implement it on a a dedicated neuromorphic hardware platform, evaluate its performance in real-world flight scenarios, and compare it to conventional artificial neural network (ANN) based control policies.
    \item Investigate the potential of SNN's internal state memory to improve control policies in partially observable markov decision processes (POMDPs), such as with incomplete state infomation environments.
    \item Develop a full end-to-end neuromorphic control system for UAVs, based on event cameras and SNNs, evalute its performance in real-world flight scenarios, and compare it to conventional ANN based control systems.
\end{itemize}
