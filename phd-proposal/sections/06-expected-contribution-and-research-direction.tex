\section{Expected Contribution and Research Direction}
\label{sec:contribution}

\subsection{Expected Contributions}
This research is expected to make two contributions, one for each objective in Section~\ref{sec:objectives}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{An end-to-end neuromorphic flight policy that operates in the wild.} A spiking policy that maps events from an onboard event camera directly to control commands, runs on a neuromorphic processor onboard a quadrotor, and flies through unknown, unstructured environments outside the laboratory, together with a method to train such policies in simulation and transfer them to real flight. Compared with a matched ANN policy, it is expected to consume less energy and, through lower perception and control latency, to fly faster.
    \item \textbf{Methods to exploit the internal memory of SNNs.} An investigation of how the internal state of an SNN, such as its membrane potentials, can be exploited as memory in a control policy. This memory is expected to improve performance in two settings: under partial observability, where the onboard sensors observe only part of the state; and under unreliable inputs, where events arrive asynchronously and with jittery, irregular timing, so the policy must integrate them over time rather than act on each in isolation.
\end{enumerate}
