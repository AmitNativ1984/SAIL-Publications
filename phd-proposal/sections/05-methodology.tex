\section{Methodology}

\subsection{Theory}
The theoretical part of this research is based on an extensive literature review in four areas: spiking neural networks; event-based processing and event representations; agile flight; and methods for training control policies.

\subsection{Simulation}
\label{subsec:simulation}
Policies will be trained in GPU-based simulators that run thousands of environments in parallel, such as the Aerial Gym Simulator~\cite{kulkarniAerialGymSimulator2025a} and NVIDIA Isaac Sim. Since the policies will observe events, these simulators will be extended with a simulation of the event camera. Before real flights, the trained policies will be tested with the full flight stack in software-in-the-loop (SITL), and possibly hardware-in-the-loop (HITL), simulation in simulators such as Gazebo and AirSim.
